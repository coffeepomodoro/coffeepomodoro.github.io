❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my data across devices?
Sign in with Google in Settings → Account. Your data will automatically sync via Firebase Cloud.
How do I upgrade to Premium?
Open the app → Settings → Premium. Choose your preferred donation tier ($3.99-$19.99) or one-time purchase.
Can I customize timer durations?
Yes! Go to click Timer. You can adjust work time, short break, and long break durations.
How do I export my statistics?
Navigate to Statistics → Menu (•••) → Export Data. Your data will be exported as CSV format.
Does the app work offline?
Yes! CoffeePomodoro works completely offline. Cloud sync is optional and only requires internet when syncing.
How do I reset my statistics?
Settings → Account → Reset Statistics. This will clear all your focus data and start fresh while keeping your account active.
How do I permanently delete my account?
To permanently delete your account and all associated data, please email us at hello@coffeepomodoro.com with subject "Account Deletion Request". We'll process your request within 48 hours and confirm deletion via email.
